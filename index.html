About the Foster

I foster cats and kittens in the San Antonio area, working with a local rescuer to help cats get the care, socialization, and safe temporary home they need while they wait for their forever families.

After these kittens find their homes, I plan to continue fostering — including adult cats who may need a little extra time and attention.

Interested in Adopting?

For adoption questions, please contact the rescue partner handling each cat's adoption. This page is for sharing their stories and helping potential adopters learn about them.
